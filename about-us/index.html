About Loke Nath Comb Factory

Home / Company Profile & Manufacturing Excellence

Est. 2017 | Bongaon, West Bengal

Small-Scale Hair Comb Manufacturing Excellence

Loke Nath Comb Factory is a dedicated small-scale manufacturing business located in Bongaon, North 24 Parganas, West Bengal, India. Specializing in high-quality hair combs and hair grooming items, the company operates as a sole proprietorship led by CEO U. Nath.

Under our product brands STYLE and ZEYU, we produce a versatile line of grooming products—including wooden combs, pocket combs, hotel amenity combs, lice combs, and professional salon styling brushes.

STYLE Brand

Durable daily household combs, family packs, and pocket combs distributed across retail markets.

ZEYU Brand

Premium professional salon brushes, wooden combs, and eco-friendly hotel amenity lines.
