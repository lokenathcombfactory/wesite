Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common OEM manufacturing & wholesale inquiry questions.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

For standard items from our Style & Zeyu brands, MOQ starts at 500 pcs. For custom OEM colors or custom printed hotel comb packaging, MOQ is typically 5,000 pcs.

Can I request product samples before bulk ordering?

Yes! We provide free physical sample packs for quality evaluation. International express courier charges apply.

Do you provide custom mold design and branding?

Yes, our engineering department designs 3D CAD molds according to your specific hair comb ergonomics and dimensions.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

We offer FOB Kolkata / Haldia Port, CIF, and door-to-door shipping options depending on your location.
